🔬 Pregnancy & HIV/STI Support

Time-critical HIV PEP guidelines, responsible pregnancy test timelines, and STI education.

Time Critical Medical Protocol

🚨 HIV PEP (Post-Exposure Prophylaxis) 72-Hour Window

PEP is a 28-day emergency medication course that can prevent HIV from establishing in your body after potential exposure. It MUST be assessed and initiated as soon as possible, and strictly within 72 hours (3 days) of exposure. Every hour counts.

Where to get PEP: Any Hospital Emergency Department (ER), Sexual Assault Referral Centre (SARC), or Urgent Care Clinic can evaluate and dispense PEP. In many jurisdictions, PEP for sexual assault survivors is provided free of charge through crime victim assistance funds.

🤰 Responsible Pregnancy Guidance & Testing Window

SurvivorCare AI does not diagnose pregnancy from symptoms. Symptoms like nausea, tenderness, or fatigue can stem from shock, stress, or emergency contraception.

📚 STI Information & Clinical Testing Timelines

Most sexually transmitted infections (STIs) are asymptomatic in the early stages. SurvivorCare AI cannot rule out infections based on symptoms. Clinical laboratory testing is required.

🔒 Quick Exit Limitations & Safety Advice

What Quick Exit does: It immediately switches your display to a benign weather and notes camouflage screen, alters the browser tab title, and clears sensitive active input forms.

Important Limitations:
  • Browser History: Web browsers still store visited addresses in history unless you use Private/Incognito mode or manually clear history. Quick Exit cannot wipe browser cache.
  • Shoulder Surfing & Spyware: If someone is physically watching your screen or has spyware/keyloggers installed on the device, Quick Exit cannot prevent them from having seen previous screens.
  • Network & Router Logs: Wi-Fi routers log domain names visited when online.

Best Practice: Use Private/Incognito browsing, and if you feel unsafe using this device, try to use a secure public library or friend's device.

🆘 Denial of Food & Water Emergency Guidance

Withholding food, water, or life-saving medication is an acute, unlawful form of abuse and physical endangerment.

  • Discreet Hydration: Tap water from bathroom sinks can sustain you. Keep small clean bottles or cups hidden in your room or laundry basket.
  • Signaling Neighbors: If you are physically confined, discreetly signal delivery drivers, postal workers, or neighbors via a window note or coded light flickers.
  • Emergency Escalation: Denial of water causes rapid biological decline within 48–72 hours. If your health is deteriorating, contact emergency services (911/112) or our 24/7 Domestic Violence hotline (1-800-799-7233) immediately.
